⚡ Grammar Arena – Passive Voice

What is the Passive Voice?
In the passive voice, we focus on what happens to something, not who does it.

Structure: Subject + am/is/are + past participle

Example: A T-shirt is made of cotton.

We use it when:
  • The action is more important than the person doing it.
  • We don't know who did it.
  • We are describing a process.

📖 Example: The Lifecycle of a Plastic Bottle

1Oil is taken from the ground.
2Oil is made into plastic pellets.
3Pellets are melted and shaped into bottles.
4Bottles are filled with water.
5Bottles are transported to stores.
6Bottles are sold to consumers.
7Bottles are used and then thrown away.
8Some bottles are recycled into new products.
